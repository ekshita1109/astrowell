Why sleep matters in space

On the space station the sun rises and sets about 16 times a day, which confuses the body clock. Good sleep helps astronauts stay alert, make safe decisions, keep a stable mood and recover physically.

    About AstroWell

    Why wellness matters in space

    Astronauts live far from home in a small space with little privacy, a changing day-night cycle and a lot of responsibility. Microgravity affects muscles, bones and sleep, while isolation can cause stress, low mood and loneliness. Looking after both physical and mental health keeps crews safe and missions successful.

    How AstroWell helps

    AstroWell brings daily check-ins into one place: mood, sleep, stress, activity and water intake. Charts make changes easy to see, and simple tools like the breathing exercise offer quick support. In the future it could be connected to a backend and database.

    This is a hackathon demo that uses sample data only. It is not connected to NASA or any real astronaut health devices and is not medical advice.
